System Types

Cladding Systems We Offer

Every building envelope is different. We offer the right system for your application, climate zone and budget.

Single-Skin Wall Cladding

High-tensile PPGI/PPGL trapezoidal or corrugated profiles in 0.47mm–0.80mm thickness. Ideal for dry industrial environments, sheds and agricultural buildings requiring an economical, durable envelope.

Insulated Sandwich Panels

Factory-bonded panels with EPS, PIR or mineral wool cores (40mm to 120mm) for superior thermal insulation, fire resistance and acoustic control. Essential for cold chain, clean rooms and air-conditioned facilities.

Louvred Ventilation Cladding

Fixed or operable aluminium louvre blades integrated into the cladding facade for natural ventilation, heat extraction and fresh air supply — critical for foundries, forge shops and high-heat industrial processes.

Translucent Polycarbonate Panels

Multi-wall polycarbonate or fibreglass translucent panels installed as wall daylight strips, reducing artificial lighting requirements by up to 40% while providing diffused natural light across the facility floor.

Parapet & Ridge Flashings

Custom-formed GI/PPGI parapet caps, ridge cappings, hip flashings and corner trims in matching RAL colours — ensuring all abutments, penetrations and roof-to-wall junctions are fully weatherproofed.

Gutters & Rainwater Systems

Sized by IS 2527 hydraulic site assessments, our box gutters, valley gutters, rainwater pipes, sump boxes and overflow weirs handle India's peak monsoon intensity without ponding or leakage.

Our Methodology

Cladding Installation Process

1

Envelope Installation

Our engineers prepare cladding layout drawings, panel schedules and flashing details based on structural grids, door/window openings, ventilation requirements and aesthetic preferences. Wind zone and thermal performance are calculated at this stage.

2

Panel Erection

Panels are pre-assembled to cut lengths with precision CNC roll-forming equipment. Insulated sandwich panels are factory-bonded under controlled conditions, ensuring consistent core density and facing adhesion. Custom trims are roll-formed to match.

3

Site Installation

Cladding panels are installed from base angle upward using MEWPs and scaffold systems. Self-drilling fasteners are torqued to specification; concealed clips are used for exposed-fastener-free elevations. Panel alignment is checked with string lines every three courses.

4

Flashings & Sealants

All openings, corners, parapets and roof-to-wall junctions receive custom-formed flashings sealed with EPDM or butyl tape. A final hose-test is conducted on critical junctions before handover to confirm a fully weather-tight envelope.

Common Questions

Industrial Cladding — FAQ

What is the difference between single-skin cladding and insulated sandwich panels?

Single-skin cladding is a single metal sheet fixed to the secondary steel girts — it provides weather protection and is the most economical option. Insulated sandwich panels have a rigid foam or mineral wool core bonded between two metal facings, providing significantly better thermal performance, acoustic reduction and fire resistance. For cold-storage, food processing, pharma or air-conditioned facilities, sandwich panels are recommended; for open industrial sheds, single-skin is usually sufficient.

How long does wall cladding installation take on a typical industrial building?

For a standard 50m × 100m single-storey industrial building, wall cladding installation typically takes 10–18 working days depending on panel type, number of openings and access conditions. Insulated panel systems with custom trims take slightly longer than single-skin profiles. We provide a project-specific installation programme at the time of quotation.

Can you match the cladding colour to our corporate branding?

Yes — we offer over 40 standard RAL and BS colours in PVDF and SMP coatings. For exact corporate colour matches (outside standard RAL range), we can source special-order material from our colour-coating partners. Colour chips and physical samples are provided before production for approval. Multiple colour combinations — for body, trims and flashings — can be coordinated to match your brand guidelines.

What is the fire rating of your sandwich panel systems?

Our mineral wool core sandwich panels achieve up to 60-minute EI (Integrity and Insulation) fire rating as tested to EN 13823 / EN 1363. These panels are factory-certified and suitable for facilities requiring compliance with IS 1641–1646 or insurance underwriter requirements for fire-resistant separation walls and facades. PIR and EPS core panels have limited fire resistance and are not suitable for rated applications.

How do you manage rainwater from the building in your cladding installation?

Rainwater management is an integral part of our cladding scope. We calculate gutter and downpipe sizes to IS 2527 based on the roof catchment area and the 10-year peak rainfall intensity for the project location. Box gutters, valley gutters, sump outlets and downpipes are included in the installation. Overflow weirs are provided at critical locations to prevent structural ponding in the event of downpipe blockage.
