Who We Are

Company Profile

RK Roofing PEB Pvt Ltd is a trusted Pan-India service provider specializing in Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) erection, structural steel erection, industrial roofing, wall cladding, and comprehensive industrial installation services.

Established in 2003 by Late Shri Raj Kishore Singh, our foundation was built on reliability, safety, and unwavering dedication to quality workmanship.

Over the past two decades, we have successfully executed critical infrastructure projects across diverse sectors, including automotive, logistics, manufacturing, airports, and commercial warehousing.

Our focus is strictly on execution, fabrication, and installation — bringing engineered precision to every construction site we manage.

What We Do

Our Services

PEB Erection

Expert erection of Pre-Engineered Buildings for industrial and commercial use. Precise assembly of primary and secondary framing systems ensuring structural integrity and longevity.

Structural Steel Erection

Safe and efficient erection of heavy structural steel for complex industrial frameworks, power plants, and large-span warehouses.

Industrial Roofing

Installation of high-grade industrial roofing solutions. We execute durable, weather-proof roofing systems tailored to exact facility requirements.

Standing Seam Roofing

Installation of advanced standing seam and Kalzip roofing systems for leak-proof, continuous surface protection on large commercial facilities.

Wall Cladding

Premium exterior wall cladding execution for aesthetic appeal and superior environmental protection of industrial structures.

Insulated Panel Services

Precision installation of PUF/EPS insulated panels to ensure optimal thermal efficiency for temperature-sensitive facilities.
