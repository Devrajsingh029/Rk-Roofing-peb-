From primary mainframes to complex process structures — our erection teams handle every element of the structural steel package.

Primary Frame Erection

Our Methodology

Erection Process — Step by Step

1

Shop Drawing Review

Erection engineers review approved shop drawings for connection details, bolt lists, camber diagrams and erection sequence before any material is loaded on transport.

2

Bolt & Weld Procedure Qualification

Welding procedure specifications (WPS) and welder qualifications (WPQ) are submitted and approved. Bolt torque values are pre-set and verified with calibrated torque wrenches.

3

Crane & Rigging Plan

A detailed lift plan — including crane radius, load charts, rigging hardware specifications and exclusion zones — is prepared by a competent lifting supervisor and approved by the project EHS officer.

4

Stage-wise Erection

Columns, rafters, bracings and secondary steel are erected bay by bay with temporary bracing at each stage. Survey checks are conducted after each bay before proceeding to the next.

5

NDT & Final Survey

Critical welds undergo UT/MPT third-party NDT. A final total-station survey confirms all columns are within permissible plumb and alignment tolerances before structural completion certificate is issued.

Common Questions

Structural Steel Erection — FAQ

What is the difference between primary and secondary steel erection?

Primary steel refers to the main load-bearing frame — columns, rafters and apex connections — that carry gravity and wind loads. Secondary steel includes purlins, girts, sag rods, bridging and eave struts that support the roof and wall cladding. RK Roofing PEB executes both under a single erection contract, eliminating coordination gaps between the two trades.

How do you ensure plumb and alignment accuracy during erection?

We use optical theodolites and digital total stations at multiple checkpoints during erection. Each column is surveyed for plumb (tolerance ±H/1000 as per IS 800), and bay diagonal measurements are taken to verify squareness. Any deviations are corrected with adjustable anchor bolts and shimming before the next erection stage begins.

Can erection proceed alongside civil and slab work?

Yes — this is one of our core advantages. With careful sequencing, structural erection can start on completed column pedestals while civil finishing (slab, grouting, apron) continues on other bays. Our project manager coordinates the interface to prevent clashes, reducing the overall project duration by typically 15–25% compared to strictly sequential construction.

What safety systems do you deploy on site?

Every erection site operates under a site-specific EHS plan that includes: permit-to-work for crane lifts, full-body harness and fall-arrest lanyard requirements above 2m, LOTO (Lockout/Tagout) for electrical interfaces, toolbox talks at shift start, first-aid station and emergency response procedure. Our riggers hold valid NCVT / NIFE certifications and undergo refresher training annually.

Do you supply the structural steel or erect only?

RK Roofing PEB offers both supply-and-erect and erect-only contracts. For supply-and-erect, we coordinate erection, installation (through our partner contractors and OEMs like Kirby, Interarch, Alfa PEB) and erection under a single complete package. For erect-only contracts, we work from the client's approved shop drawings and fabricated material — providing cranes, rigging gear, consumables and labour.
