Why Hyderabad

Hyderabad's Industrial Growth & PEB Opportunity

Hyderabad has emerged as one of India's fastest-growing industrial and logistics destinations. Beyond its status as a global IT hub, Greater Hyderabad hosts high-quality pharmaceutical parks including the Hyderabad Pharma City at Genome Valley — poised to be the world's largest pharmaceutical manufacturing cluster — as well as robust industrial development zones at IDA Pashamylaram, Patancheru, Bollaram, Medchal and HMDA's Shamshabad corridor near the Rajiv Gandhi International Airport. These zones are generating extraordinary demand for large-scale PEB manufacturing plants, cleanroom facilities, logistics warehouses and ancillary industrial buildings.

The Telangana government's proactive TS-iPASS industrial policy and high-quality industrial infrastructure have attracted massive investments from Bharat Biotech, Dr. Reddy's Laboratories, Amazon, Flipkart and dozens of global manufacturers, all requiring rapid industrial construction. Pre-Engineered Buildings — with their speed of erection, cost efficiency and installation flexibility — are the preferred construction method across Hyderabad's industrial zones.

RK Roofing PEB Pvt Ltd brings proven expertise in PEB construction and industrial roofing to Hyderabad clients. Working with authorised partners Kirby, Interarch, Alfa PEB and Zamil, we source the right building system for each application — whether a pharmaceutical-grade cleanroom shell, a high-cube logistics warehouse or a heavy-duty manufacturing shed with 30-tonne overhead cranes.
