Our Story

About RK Roofing PEB Pvt Ltd

RK Roofing was established in 2003 by Late Shri Raj Kishore Singh, whose vision, leadership, and dedication laid the foundation for the company's success. In its early years, the company focused on industrial roofing and related services, earning a reputation for quality workmanship, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

In 2009, RK Roofing expanded its operations into Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) Erection, structural steel erection, and industrial installation services. This strategic expansion enabled the company to undertake larger and more complex projects across a wide range of industries throughout India.

Over the years, RK Roofing has successfully executed projects across diverse sectors, including automotive, warehousing, logistics parks, manufacturing plants, power, airports, aircraft hangars, cement, steel, cold storage facilities, industrial, and commercial infrastructure.

Today, RK Roofing PEB Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted Pan-India service provider specializing in PEB erection, structural steel erection, industrial roofing, wall cladding, insulated panel installation, standing seam roofing systems, Kalzip roofing systems, mezzanine floors, cold storage solutions, maintenance, and repair services.
