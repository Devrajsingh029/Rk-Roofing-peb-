About RK Roofing PEB Pvt Ltd

RK Roofing PEB Pvt Ltd is a trusted Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) company specializing in industrial roofing systems, structural steel erection, warehouse construction, roofing and cladding solutions, and turnkey industrial infrastructure projects.

Since our foundation, we have successfully delivered industrial buildings, logistics parks, automobile facilities, warehouses, manufacturing units, and infrastructure developments across India.

Our commitment to engineering excellence, quality workmanship, safety, and timely project delivery has made us a trusted construction partner for industrial and commercial clients throughout Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and India.

Trusted Pre-Engineered Building Company for Industrial Construction Projects

RK Roofing PEB Pvt Ltd is recognized as a trusted Pre-Engineered Building Company serving industrial, commercial and infrastructure clients across India. Our in-house team delivers integrated design, fabrication and engineering services.

As a premier Warehouse Construction Company in Karnataka, we plan, design, and execute Grade-A warehouse developments — from civil and PEB superstructure to roofing, cladding, and finishes. We support 3PL operators, distribution hubs and manufacturers with single-window accountability and engineering rigor.

Our expertise in industrial roofing systems includes premium roofing engineered for Indian operating conditions — bare galvalume, colour-coating, insulated panels and standing-seam solutions. We integrate roofing and cladding expertise across the PEB project, delivering accountable single-source execution.

Our Structural Steel Erection Services cover heavy mainframes, beam and crane structures for industrial sheds, manufacturing plants, and cement and steel facilities. We are the preferred sub-contractor for many PEB Structure Installations and Industrial Shed Constructions, regardless of the scope's complexity or geography.

From compact industrial sheds to large-format logistics parks, we serve as full-service Steel Building Contractors delivering Turnkey Industrial Construction Solutions. Whether you are planning new Warehouse Infrastructure Development or upgrading existing facilities, we make it easy to engineer and build with reliability, safety, and scale.
