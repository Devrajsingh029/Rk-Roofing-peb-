Why Hosur

Hosur — South India's Fastest-Growing Industrial Town

Hosur, situated just 40 km from Bengaluru on the Tamil Nadu–Karnataka border, has transformed into one of South India's most vibrant industrial corridors. The presence of Tata Electronics, TVS Motor Company, Hindustan Unilever and a host of EV battery manufacturers has turned Hosur into a hub of advanced manufacturing. With the Bangalore–Chennai Expressway now operational and SIPCOT industrial estates expanding rapidly, demand for large-span PEB structures — warehouses, assembly sheds, logistics parks and ancillary facilities — has never been higher.

RK Roofing PEB Pvt Ltd has established a strong footprint in Hosur over the past decade, delivering some of the region's most prominent industrial structures. Our proximity in Bangalore allows rapid mobilization to Hosur sites, and our deep familiarity with Krishnagiri District's DTCP and SIPCOT building norms ensures smooth approvals and hassle-free project execution.

Our portfolio in Hosur includes the landmark Horizon Logistics Park — a multi-span, high-cube warehouse complex — and the Welspun One logistics facility, both delivered to international specifications and tight timelines. These projects demonstrate our capability to handle complex multi-bay PEB structures with integrated dock systems, fire suppression provisions and high-specification industrial roofing.
