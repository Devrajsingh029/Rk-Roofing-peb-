Key Benefits

Why steel factory sheds outperform RCC construction

Quick Erection (4–8 Weeks)

pre-assembled components arrive on site ready to bolt together. A typical 5,000 sqm factory shed superstructure is erected in 4 to 8 weeks — versus 12+ months for RCC equivalent — dramatically reducing time-to-production.

Large Clear Spans

PEB rigid frames achieve clear spans of 15m to 90m without intermediate columns — enabling unrestricted production floor layouts, flexible material flow paths and future equipment reconfiguration.

Heavy Crane Compatibility

PEB column sections are designed to accept EOT crane runway beams (mono or double-girder) for capacities up to 50 tonnes — all within the same integrated structural system from foundation anchor bolts to crane rail level.

Natural Ventilation

Ridge ventilators, louvred gable monitors and wall ventilators integrated into the PEB structure provide effective cross-ventilation for hot industrial environments without costly mechanical HVAC systems.

Fire-Safe Installation

Structural steel members are designed with adequate fire rating provisions, intumescent paint options and smoke vent provisions compliant with NBC 2016 industrial occupancy requirements.

Low Lifecycle Cost

Lower initial construction cost, minimal structural maintenance over a 30+ year life, re-usable components and easy future extension make PEB factory sheds the most cost-effective industrial building choice in India.

Our Process

From brief to built — four stages.

Our streamlined factory shed delivery process is designed to get your production facility operational as quickly as possible — without cutting corners on quality or safety.

01

Site Analysis

We visit your site to assess ground conditions, production layout requirements, EOT crane paths, ventilation needs and statutory setbacks. A budgetary estimate and preliminary specification are provided within 3 working days.

02

Steel Structure Erection

Computer-aided structural analysis generates optimised tapered frame sections. Crane girder installation, purlin layout, bracing pattern and foundation anchor bolt sizes are finalised and submitted for client approval before erection commences.

03

Steel Erection

Primary frames are fabricated at the partner manufacturer's facility using CNC plasma cutting, automatic submerged arc welding and 100% dimensional inspection. Primer application and factory inspection are completed before dispatch to site.

04

Erection & Finishing

Certified erection crews raise frames, install purlins, fix roofing and wall cladding, fit crane rails and accessories, apply touch-up coatings and complete all finishing work. As-built drawings and material certificates are submitted at handover.

Frequently Asked Questions

Factory shed questions answered

Why should I choose a steel factory shed over RCC construction?

Steel PEB factory sheds are erected 4–5 times faster than RCC construction, use 20–30% less structural material, allow column-free spans up to 90m, are easily expandable without demolition and have a lower lifecycle cost over 30+ years. For manufacturers needing early operational dates, the faster construction speed alone justifies the choice.

Can your factory sheds accommodate EOT cranes?

Yes. EOT crane compatibility is one of our core installation capabilities. We installation the PEB columns and haunches to support crane runway beams for single-girder cranes up to 10T and double-girder cranes up to 50T. Runway beam installation, end stops, buffer systems and crane-specific column stiffeners are all integrated into the Steel Structure Erection from the start.

How do you ensure the factory shed is well-ventilated?

Effective ventilation for factory sheds involves ridge ventilators (continuous or hit-and-miss), gable-end louvres, elevated monitor roofs, wall ventilators at low level and polycarbonate skylight strips for natural daylight. We calculate ventilation area requirements based on occupancy, heat generation from equipment and prevailing wind direction — all integrated into the PEB structure installation at no extra structural cost.

Can I expand my factory shed in the future?

Absolutely — and we plan for this from day one. PEB end walls are designed with expansion anchors in the foundation and the end rafter is specified to accept a new frame when additional bays are needed. Longitudinal expansion is as simple as adding new bays at either end of the existing building without disturbing ongoing production.

What is the cost of a factory shed per sqft in India?

PEB factory shed superstructure (primary frame, secondary steel, roofing and basic wall cladding) typically costs ₹1,200 to ₹1,800 per sqft at current market prices, depending on span, height, crane provision and roofing specification. Complete costs including civil foundation vary significantly with soil conditions and local material rates. Contact us for a site-specific estimate.
