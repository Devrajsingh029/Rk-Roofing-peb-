Why Coimbatore

Coimbatore — South India's Manufacturing Capital

Coimbatore is justifiably known as the "Manchester of South India" — a city where textiles, precision engineering, pump manufacturing and foundries have flourished for over a century. Today, Coimbatore's industrial ecosystem has expanded to include IT manufacturing, agri-processing, auto-components and a rapidly growing logistics sector supported by the Coimbatore International Airport expansion and improved connectivity via NH-544. The city's SIDCO and SIPCOT industrial estates, including the major SIPCOT Perundurai complex and Coimbatore's own Peelamedu and Kurichi industrial clusters, are witnessing a wave of new factory construction as manufacturers expand capacity.

Pre-Engineered Buildings are transforming industrial construction in Coimbatore. Textile mills upgrading their weaving sheds, pump manufacturers building new test facilities, and logistics companies establishing distribution hubs are all turning to PEB for its speed, cost efficiency and installation flexibility.
