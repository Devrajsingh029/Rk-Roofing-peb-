Why Bangalore

Bengaluru's Industrial Landscape & RK Roofing PEB's Role

Bengaluru is India's technology and manufacturing powerhouse, home to aerospace giants like HAL and DRDO, automotive clusters in Bommasandra and Peenya, and a rapidly expanding logistics and warehousing corridor stretching from Whitefield to Yelahanka and Devanahalli. The city's industrial estates — KIADB Aerospace Park, Electronic City Phase I & II, and the Bangalore-Tumkur industrial corridor — generate continuous demand for large-span Pre-Engineered Buildings that can be erected quickly without disrupting operations.

RK Roofing PEB Pvt Ltd is headquartered at Yelahanka New Town, Bengaluru — placing our project management team, erection crews and material storage within minutes of Bangalore's fastest-growing industrial zones. Since our founding in 2000, we have supplied and erected PEB structures, industrial roofing systems and complete warehouse buildings across every major industrial cluster in the city, including Yelahanka, Hebbal, Peenya, Bommasandra, Electronic City, Whitefield and Devanahalli.

Our notable Bangalore portfolio includes the Alfa PEB manufacturing facility in Yelahanka, structural roofing works for the BMRCL Metro infrastructure, and large-span warehouse covers at Bangalore Palace premises. Being a local company means faster mobilization, lower logistics costs, and a team that understands Karnataka's building code and BBMP/BMRDA permit requirements firsthand.
