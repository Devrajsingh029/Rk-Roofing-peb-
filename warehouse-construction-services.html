Warehouse Construction Specifications

Standard installation parameters for Grade-A warehouse projects. All specifications customised to site conditions and operational requirements.

Parameter Specification
Clear Span 30m to 60m (single span); multi-span for wider footprints
Eave Height 8m to 15m (Grade-A standard minimum 10m for racking)
Floor Load 5 to 10 tonnes/sqm (built to client's racking and forklift loads)
Dock Levellers Hydraulic dock levellers as required; dock shelter and bumper included
Column Spacing 6m to 12m bay spacing (optimised for racking layout)
Flooring CC M30 with FM2 flatness (3mm / 3m tolerance) or Industrial Epoxy topping
Fire Compliance NBC 2016 — provisions for sprinkler, fire extinguishers, hydrant and smoke vents
Our Process

Six stages to your operational warehouse.

A disciplined project management approach that keeps your warehouse on schedule from first survey to keys-in-hand handover.

01

Feasibility

Site visit, soil investigation review, statutory clearance check and initial concept layout. We provide a budgetary estimate and preliminary project schedule within 5 working days of your brief.

02

Installation & BOQ

Structural engineering, architectural drawings, PEB installation co-ordination, detailed Bill of Quantities and final contract. All client and statutory approvals secured before mobilisation.

03

Civil & Foundation

Earthworks, sub-grade preparation, concrete column foundations, plinth beams and industrial floor slab (M30 CC with FM2 flatness) executed in parallel with PEB erection to compress the programme.

04

PEB Erection

Primary rigid frames, secondary purlins and girts, bracing systems and structural accessories erected to IS 800 tolerances by our certified erection crews using calibrated torque equipment.

05

MEP & Finishes

Roofing, wall cladding, insulation, dock levellers, fire protection provisions, LED lighting, electrical distribution, rainwater systems and all finish works completed and commissioned.

06

Handover

Final snagging inspection, commissioning of all systems, statutory completion certificate, as-built drawings, material certificates and O&M manuals handed over. Defect liability period begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Warehouse construction questions answered

What is a Grade-A warehouse and why does it matter?

Grade-A warehouses meet a defined set of physical standards: minimum 10m clear height, dock-height loading (1.2m above ground), 5–10 T/sqm floor loading, large truck aprons, 24/7 security provisions and fire compliance to NBC 2016. These specifications are mandatory for leading 3PL operators, e-commerce companies and MNCs, and attract premium rental yields for developers.

How long does warehouse construction take?

A typical 50,000 sqft Grade-A PEB warehouse (civil, superstructure, roofing, cladding and basic MEP) takes 4 to 6 months from project approval. A larger 1,00,000 sqft project with full fit-out takes 6 to 9 months. We share a detailed programme at the BOQ stage so clients can plan lease-up and operations in advance.

Do you construct warehouses for lease or only for owner-occupiers?

We build for both. For developers constructing speculative or build-to-suit warehouses for lease to 3PL operators, we can installation to Grade-A standards from day one. For owner-occupiers who need a bespoke facility for their own operations, we customise layout, door positions, flooring spec and MEP to their specific process requirements.

Can you add dock levellers and fire protection to an existing warehouse?

Yes. We retrofit dock levellers, dock shelters, fire hydrant systems, sprinkler infrastructure, LED high-bay lighting and rainwater management to existing warehouses — planned around your operational schedule to minimise downtime. Our engineers assess the existing structure first to ensure all additions are structurally compatible.

What is the construction cost per sqft for a Grade-A warehouse in India?

Construction cost depends heavily on location, soil conditions, specification level (basic vs full fit-out) and market material prices. As a general guide, a Grade-A PEB warehouse superstructure (excluding civil foundation) typically costs ₹1,500 to ₹2,200 per sqft at current market rates. Contact us for a site-specific estimate — we provide detailed BOQs at no cost.
