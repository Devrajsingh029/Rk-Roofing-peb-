About the Service

We specialize in the erection and installation of large-span steel structures specifically engineered for aircraft hangars. Our teams execute complex roof profiles, specialized cladding, and thermal insulation to meet stringent aviation standards and accommodate large clear-span requirements without intermediate columns.

Services Included

Aircraft Hangar Erection

Professional erection of massive steel frameworks engineered to meet stringent aviation standards and accommodate large aircraft securely.

Large-Span Steel Structures

Installation of clear-span structural steel installations that eliminate intermediate columns, providing maximum unhindered floor space.

Aviation Infrastructure Roofing

Application of specialized weather-resistant roofing systems tailored for airport infrastructure, ensuring long-term durability against extreme elements.

Hangar Cladding & Insulation

High-performance wall cladding and integrated thermal insulation systems that regulate hangar temperatures and protect aviation assets.
