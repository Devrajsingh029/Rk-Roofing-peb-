Roofing Systems

Six industrial roofing solutions for every application

We select and install the right system based on your facility's temperature requirements, span, rainfall zone and budget.

Bare Galvalume Roofing

AZ150 Galvalume steel sheets provide excellent corrosion resistance and thermal reflectivity at a competitive cost. Ideal for general manufacturing and storage facilities where insulation is not critical.

Colour-Coated Roofing

PVDF or SMP colour-coated Galvalume sheets with 10–15 year colour-fade warranty. Superior corrosion resistance for coastal and chemically aggressive industrial environments.

Standing Seam Roofing

Mechanically seamed concealed-fix profile eliminates exposed fasteners — the primary source of roof leaks. Suitable for low-slope roofs (1°–3°) and large industrial buildings requiring long sheet runs.

Insulated Sandwich Panels

PIR or EPS core panels with GI/Galvalume steel skins deliver R-values of 2.0–4.0 m²K/W. Mandatory for cold stores, food processing units and air-conditioned manufacturing facilities.

Trapezoidal Profile Sheets

Hi-rib and lo-rib trapezoidal profiles in 0.47mm to 0.80mm base metal thickness for spans of 1.5m to 3.0m between purlins. Cost-effective for large roof areas with standard pitch.

Polycarbonate Skylights

Multiwall polycarbonate ridge and bay skylights providing 10–15% daylight factor, reducing artificial lighting costs by up to 60% in daytime. UV-stabilised for 10+ year light transmission warranty.

Four steps to a leak-free industrial roof.

01

Our engineers conduct a comprehensive roof survey — measuring spans, assessing existing purlins, measuring rainfall intensity, checking for existing leaks and identifying structural loads before any recommendation is made.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common industrial roofing questions

What is the best roofing sheet for Indian industrial buildings?

For most industrial facilities in India, 0.50mm BMT AZ150 Galvalume colour-coated sheets with PVDF finish offer the best balance of cost, durability and corrosion resistance. In high-rainfall or coastal zones, we recommend thicker gauge (0.63mm) sheets. For temperature-controlled environments, PIR insulated sandwich panels are the correct choice.

How do you fix industrial roof leaks without shutting down production?

We plan all roofing retrofit and repair work in sections, working bay by bay to minimise the area exposed at any time. Tarpaulin protection, early-morning start times and monsoon scheduling ensure your production is protected during re-roofing. Emergency leak repair teams are available on short notice for active leaks.

What is standing seam roofing and when should I use it?

Standing seam roofing is a concealed-fix system where adjacent sheets are mechanically folded together at the seam, eliminating all exposed fasteners. It is ideal for very low slopes (1°–3°), long sheet runs and buildings where roof leaks would cause serious production or product damage — such as electronics, pharmaceutical and food processing facilities.

How much insulation do I need for an industrial roof in South India?

For non-air-conditioned facilities in South India, a 50mm glass wool blanket (density 16 kg/m³) under colour-coated sheets reduces internal temperature by 6–10°C. For air-conditioned or cold-chain environments, 75mm to 150mm PIR sandwich panels (U-value 0.25 to 0.15 W/m²K) are recommended to meet NBC energy conservation provisions.

Can you re-roof an existing industrial building without replacing the structure?

Yes. If the existing purlin structure is sound (checked by our engineers), we can overlay new colour-coated sheets or standing-seam panels directly over the old roof, or strip and re-sheet as appropriate. This avoids costly structural replacement and is typically 40% more economical than full roof replacement. We carry out structural assessment before recommending the approach.
