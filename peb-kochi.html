Kochi's Industrial Growth Story

Kochi is Kerala's economic engine — home to the Cochin Port and Vallarpadam International Container Transshipment Terminal (ICTT), which handles a significant share of Kerala's export-import trade. The city and its surroundings host a dynamic ecosystem of logistics operators, freight forwarders, cold-chain companies, and manufacturing units that depend on high-quality industrial buildings.

The Kerala Industrial Infrastructure Development Corporation (Kinfra) operates multiple industrial parks in the Kochi region, including Kinfra Film and Video Park, Kinfra International Apparel Park (Aroor), and the Kalamassery Industrial Estate — home to Kerala's pharmaceutical and precision engineering clusters. Thrissur, Aluva, and Perumbavoor are adjacent industrial nodes within easy reach.

RK Roofing PEB Pvt Ltd, with its South India operations and national supply network, is well-positioned to serve Kochi's diverse industrial landscape. Our PEB structures are engineered for Kerala's high-humidity, high-rainfall coastal environment — with corrosion-resistant coatings, anti-condensation systems, and robust monsoon drainage installation built in as standard.

Frequently Asked

FAQs — PEB Construction in Kochi

How do you address corrosion in Kochi's coastal environment for PEB structures?

Coastal environments accelerate steel corrosion significantly. For Kochi projects, we specify Zincalume (AZ150 coating) or Galvalume roofing sheets with PVDF or SMP protective colour coatings that resist salt-laden air. Structural steel members are protected with hot-dip galvanising or high-build epoxy coating systems. Roof-to-wall seals, gutter details, and fastener specifications are all reviewed for coastal durability.

Can RK Roofing PEB build warehouses near Cochin Port and ICTT?

Yes. We build warehouses and logistics facilities in the Kochi port vicinity, including container freight station (CFS) buildings, bulk commodity storage, and customs-bonded warehouses. Our structures meet the structural, fire safety, and waterproofing requirements for port-zone operations. We also coordinate with local authorities for statutory approvals.

What Kinfra parks do you serve in the Kochi region?

We serve Kinfra industrial parks across Ernakulam and Thrissur districts — including Kalamassery, Aroor, Irinjalakuda, and other KSIDC/Kinfra-notified industrial estates. We are familiar with Kinfra building approval processes and can assist plot allottees with structural drawings and project execution from ground-breaking to handover.

How does PEB perform during Kerala's extreme monsoon season?

Kerala receives some of India's highest annual rainfall — up to 3,000mm in Kochi's environs. Our PEB roofing installations for Kerala incorporate steeper roof slopes (minimum 1:10 to 1:6), oversized gutters and downspouts, fully sealed lap joints with butyl tape, valley-flashing at every bay junction, and Kochi-specific wind load site assessments per IS:875. These measures ensure completely leak-proof performance through even the heaviest monsoon events.
