Pre-Engineered Buildings
What is a Pre-Engineered Building (PEB)?
PEB

A Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) is a steel structure that is designed, fabricated, and supplied as a complete kit from a factory, then erected on-site. Primary frames, secondary members, roofing, and cladding are all engineered for the specific load conditions of your project. PEB construction is faster, more cost-effective, and highly flexible compared to conventional construction — making it the preferred choice for warehouses, factories, aircraft hangars, and large commercial facilities.

How long does it take to build a Pre-Engineered Building?
PEB

The timeline for a PEB project depends on size and complexity. Design and fabrication typically takes 6–10 weeks after drawings are approved. Erection on-site generally takes 4–12 weeks for a standard single-span warehouse. A typical 10,000–50,000 sq ft facility can be ready for handover within 4–6 months from contract signing — 30–40% faster than conventional RCC construction.

What clear spans are possible with PEB construction?
PEB

PEB frames can achieve clear spans of up to 90 metres without any intermediate columns, though most industrial applications use spans of 20–60 metres. Multi-span buildings can extend to any width by adding interior columns. RK Roofing PEB works with leading manufacturers including Kirby, Interarch, and Alfa PEB to deliver the optimal span configuration for your project requirements.

How much does a Pre-Engineered Building cost in India?
PEB

PEB construction costs in India typically range from ₹1,800 to ₹3,500+ per sq ft, depending on span, height, floor loading, crane provisions, cladding type, and geographic location. Supply-only costs for the steel structure can start from ₹900–₹1,200 per sq ft. RK Roofing PEB provides detailed, itemised quotations after understanding your specific requirements — contact us for a free, no-obligation estimate.

What design codes and standards does RK Roofing PEB follow?
PEB

RK Roofing PEB and its manufacturing partners design all structures in accordance with IS 800 (General Construction in Steel), IS 875 Parts 1–5 (Code of Practice for Design Loads), IS 1893 (Criteria for Earthquake Resistant Design), and NBC 2016. All erection activities comply with relevant IS safety codes. Designs are reviewed and certified by qualified structural engineers.

Industrial Roofing
What roofing systems does RK Roofing PEB offer?
Roofing

We offer a comprehensive range of industrial roofing systems including concealed-fix standing seam roofing (KS1000 RW and equivalent profiles), through-fix trapezoidal profiles (Trimdeck, IBR, corrugated), insulated roof panels (PIR/PUR sandwich panels), and translucent GRP skylights. All systems are available in Zincalume, colour-coated GI, and Aluzinc substrates with 0.47mm to 0.70mm BMT.

How does RK Roofing PEB prevent roof leaks?
Roofing

Leak prevention starts at design stage — we specify correct pitch (minimum 3° for standing seam, 5° for through-fix), proper laps, and valley drainage. During installation, all penetrations, ridges, and valleys are sealed with compatible butyl tape and silicone. We use stainless-steel or neoprene-washer fasteners to prevent corrosion-induced leaks. All completed roofs undergo a visual inspection and, where specified, a water test before handover.

What thickness of roofing sheet do you recommend?
Roofing

For industrial applications, we typically specify 0.50mm BMT (Base Metal Thickness) as a minimum for standard spans, and 0.60mm–0.70mm BMT for longer purlin spans or high-wind zones. Insulated sandwich panel cores range from 50mm to 100mm depending on thermal performance requirements. We follow manufacturers' load span tables to select the appropriate profile and thickness for every project.

Can RK Roofing PEB repair or re-roof an existing industrial building?
Roofing

Yes. We regularly undertake roof replacement and over-roofing projects. For over-roofing, a new steel sub-framing system is erected over the existing structure and new sheeting is installed without disrupting operations below. For re-roofing, old sheets are replaced and any corroded purlins are repaired or replaced. We also provide leak repair services and roof coating/painting to extend the life of existing roofs.

How long does a roofing installation project take?
Roofing

A typical 10,000 sq ft industrial roofing installation takes 5–10 working days with an experienced crew. Larger projects of 50,000–1,00,000 sq ft can take 3–6 weeks depending on access, crew size, and weather windows. We plan phased installations to minimise disruption to ongoing operations where required.

Warehouse Construction
What is a Grade-A warehouse specification?
Warehouses

Grade-A warehouses are premium logistics facilities designed to international standards. Key specifications include: minimum 12m clear height (often 14m–16m for racking), column-free spans of 24m or more, floor flatness of FM2 or better (<5mm variation in 3m), floor loading of 5 tonnes/sqm or more, dock-height platforms (1.2m) with dock levellers, fire sprinkler systems, CCTV, and high-lumens LED lighting. RK Roofing PEB delivers the structural and roofing components of Grade-A facilities.

What clear height can RK Roofing PEB achieve in a warehouse?
Warehouses

We can design and erect warehouse structures to any clear height, with most projects ranging from 8m to 18m under eave. For large-format 3PL and e-commerce fulfilment facilities, we have delivered 14m–16m clear-height structures. The column eave height and frame geometry are designed in conjunction with racking layouts, sprinkler systems, and material-handling equipment requirements.

Do you include dock levellers and loading docks in your warehouse projects?
Warehouses

RK Roofing PEB provides the complete structural and roofing envelope including dock pit framing and dock-height wall panels. Dock levellers (hydraulic or mechanical), dock shelters, and dock bumpers are typically procured and installed in coordination with specialist dock equipment suppliers. We coordinate with these vendors as part of our overall project management role to ensure seamless integration.

Can you build cold-chain and temperature-controlled warehouses?
Warehouses

Yes. We supply and erect the insulated building envelope for cold-chain facilities, including PIR/PUR insulated wall and roof panels with U-values as low as 0.15 W/m²K. We work closely with cold-room and refrigeration system contractors to ensure the structural design accommodates hanging loads from refrigeration equipment, vapour barriers, and all penetrations. Floor insulation and slab design are coordinated with specialist cold-room flooring contractors.

How long does it take to build a 50,000 sq ft warehouse?
Warehouses

A 50,000 sq ft PEB warehouse typically takes 5–7 months from contract signing to handover. This includes 2–3 weeks for design and approval, 8–10 weeks for fabrication and material procurement, 6–8 weeks for civil foundation work (running parallel), and 6–10 weeks for steel erection, roofing, cladding, and finishes. Timelines vary based on site conditions, permit lead times, and scope of civil works.

What is RK Roofing PEB's quality assurance process?
Process

Quality is managed at every stage. At design stage, all drawings are reviewed and approved by our engineering team before release for fabrication. At the factory, our partner manufacturers maintain ISO 9001-certified production processes with mill test certificates for all steel. On-site, our supervisors conduct daily inspections of bolt torque, alignment, plumb, and weld quality. All completed structures undergo a pre-handover inspection, and a snag list is closed before project sign-off.

How does RK Roofing PEB manage site safety?
Process

Safety is non-negotiable at RK Roofing PEB. All site workers are equipped with appropriate PPE — harnesses, helmets, safety boots, and gloves. Working-at-height procedures follow IS 3696 and applicable state factory rules. We conduct toolbox talks before every shift. Our supervisors are trained in EHS (Environmental Health & Safety) procedures. HIRA (Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment) is conducted at project commencement, and a site safety plan is maintained throughout.

Does RK Roofing PEB provide as-built drawings after project completion?
Process

Yes. We provide a complete as-built drawing package on project completion, including general arrangement drawings, foundation layout, anchor bolt plans, purlin and girt layout, and roofing/cladding layout. These are delivered in PDF and, where agreed, DWG/DXF format. As-built documents are essential for future extensions, maintenance planning, and statutory compliance records.
