What is a Pre-Engineered Building (PEB)?

A Pre-Engineered Building is a steel structure designed, fabricated and supplied as a complete kit from a single manufacturer, then erected on site. PEB systems use optimised tapered sections, cold-formed purlins and bolted connections, which makes them faster to build, lighter and more cost-efficient than conventional steel buildings â€” ideal for warehouses, factories and logistics parks across India.

What is the difference between PEB and conventional steel buildings?

PEB structures are pre-designed using built-up tapered sections that match the load curve exactly, so steel consumption is typically 20â€“30% lower than conventional hot-rolled buildings. They are also factory-fabricated to tighter tolerances, shipped flat-packed, and erected faster on site, which reduces overall project cost and schedule risk.
