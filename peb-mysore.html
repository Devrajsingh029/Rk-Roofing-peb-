Why Mysore

Mysore's Industrial Landscape & Growing PEB Demand

Mysuru, Karnataka's second-largest city, has long been an important manufacturing centre with deep roots in textile production, food processing, incense manufacturing and precision engineering. Today, the city's industrial map is expanding rapidly under KIADB's management, with Hebbal Industrial Area, Belagola Industrial Estate, Nanjangud Industrial Area and the KRS Road industrial corridor hosting hundreds of factories and warehouses that are transitioning to Pre-Engineered Building technology for faster construction and lower lifecycle costs.

The Bengaluru–Mysuru Expressway, now fully operational, has dramatically reduced travel time between the two cities and opened Mysuru to large-scale logistics and distribution investments. Industrial players from Bengaluru are increasingly setting up satellite manufacturing and warehousing units in Mysuru, driving strong demand for competitively priced, high-quality PEB structures with robust roofing systems designed for Mysuru's climate.
