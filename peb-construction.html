What Is PEB?

The smarter way to build industrial structures

A Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) is a complete steel structural system installed, erected and supplied as an optimised kit, then bolted together on site. Unlike conventional RCC or hot-rolled steel construction, PEB uses built-up tapered sections that are engineered precisely to the load demands of every frame — eliminating dead weight and reducing steel consumption by 20–30%.

At RK Roofing PEB, we partner with leading PEB manufacturers — Kirby, Interarch, Alfa PEB, Fabex, Phenix and Zamil — to supply primary frames, secondary purlins, girts, roofing and cladding as a fully coordinated system. Our in-house erection teams handle bolted connections, crane erection and structural alignment to site tolerances that meet IS 800 requirements.

Why Choose PEB

Key benefits of Pre-Engineered Building construction

Cost-Efficient Installation

Tapered primary frames optimised by computer-aided installation eliminate excess steel, reducing material cost by 20–30% versus conventional hot-rolled sections.

Faster Construction

pre-assembled components arrive on site ready to bolt together, cutting erection time by up to 40% compared to site-welded conventional frames.

Flexible Spans up to 90m

PEB rigid frame technology enables clear spans up to 90 metres with no intermediate columns, maximising usable floor area and operational flexibility.

Weather-Resistant Structure

Hot-dip galvanised bolted connections, anti-corrosive primer and colour-coated roofing ensure the structure resists India's tropical monsoon and coastal conditions.

IS 800 Compliant

All Steel Structure Erections comply with IS 800:2007 (General Construction in Steel) and IS 875 for wind and live loads, with seismic zone-specific installation inputs.

Expandable & Scalable

PEB end walls and expansion anchors are built for future bays to be added without dismantling existing structure — ideal for growing industrial facilities.

Our engineers visit the site to understand topography, soil bearing capacity, prevailing wind direction and any statutory setback requirements before a single drawing is raised.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about PEB construction in India

What is a PEB structure?

A Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) is a steel structural system built, fabricated at a factory and shipped to site as a bolted-together kit. The primary rigid frames use built-up tapered I-sections optimised by computer analysis to place steel only where loads demand it, resulting in lighter, faster and more economical structures than conventional hot-rolled frames.

How long does PEB erection take?

A typical 10,000 sqm warehouse PEB superstructure (frames, purlins, roofing and wall cladding) takes 6 to 10 weeks to erect once the foundation is ready. Timelines vary based on building size, number of cranes deployed, site access and local weather conditions. Our project schedule is shared at the BOQ stage.

What spans are possible with PEB?

PEB rigid frames can achieve clear spans from 15 metres up to 90 metres in a single-span configuration. For wider footprints, multi-span frames with interior columns bring unlimited building widths. Lean-to frames and asymmetric gable installations are also possible for sites with specific constraints.

What codes does RK Roofing PEB follow?

All PEB structures are built to IS 800:2007 (General Construction in Steel), IS 875 Parts 1–5 for dead, live, wind and seismic loads, and IS 1893 for seismic installation. Weld inspection follows IS 816 and IS 9595. Material certificates for steel plates and sections are provided with every project as standard.
