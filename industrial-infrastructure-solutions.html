Our Methodology

Complete Delivery Process

1

Project Scoping

We conduct a detailed scoping workshop with your team to understand the facility purpose, operational requirements, site constraints, utility interfaces and statutory approval status. A comprehensive project brief is prepared, capturing all scope inclusions, exclusions, interfaces and client-supply items.

2

Detailed Engineering

Structural engineering (civil, steel, roofing), architectural layouts, MEP installation interfaces and PEB installation are all progressed in parallel using our integrated installation model. All engineering deliverables are issued for your review and approval before procurement or construction begins.

3

Procurement

Bulk material orders are placed immediately after installation approval to lock in prices and secure delivery slots. PEB frames from our OEM partners typically require 8–12 weeks lead time; this is front-loaded into the schedule to avoid structural delays. MTCs and quality records are obtained for all major structural materials.

4

Construction

Civil works, structural erection, roofing and cladding are executed concurrently where the programme permits. Daily site reports and weekly progress photographs are shared with you. Our project manager chairs a weekly site co-ordination meeting with all sub-contractors to resolve any emerging issues before they impact the programme.

5

Quality & Safety

Structured QA/QC inspection hold points are enforced at each construction phase. A full EHS management plan, daily toolbox talks, permit-to-work systems and incident reporting are maintained throughout the project. Third-party NDT inspections are conducted on all critical structural welds.

6

Commissioning & Handover

A formal snag list is compiled during a joint walkthrough, all items are cleared and the project is formally handed over with a complete documentation package — including as-built drawings, structural engineer's completion certificate, material test reports, paint system records, warranty certificates and operation & maintenance guidance.

Common Questions

Industrial Infrastructure Solutions — FAQ

What is the minimum project size you handle on a comprehensive basis?

We typically take on industrial infrastructure solutions from ₹1 crore upward. Smaller projects below this threshold are generally better executed as individual trade contracts — roofing only, erection only, etc. — which we also offer. For greenfield industrial facilities, typical complete project values range from ₹3 crore to ₹50 crore depending on size, specification and scope.

Does complete include getting statutory approvals from local authorities?

Typically, statutory plan approvals (building plan sanction, environmental clearances, etc.) are obtained by the client with the assistance of a local architect/liaison agent. RK Roofing PEB provides all required structural drawings, specifications and engineer's certificates needed for submissions. Once approvals are in hand, we manage the full construction and completion phase under our complete contract.

How do you handle installation changes during construction?

Installation changes post-contract are managed through a formal variation order (VO) process. We assess the scope, cost and programme impact of any proposed change and issue a formal VO proposal for your approval before any work is executed. This provides full cost and schedule transparency for changes initiated at any stage of the project. We actively flag any client-initiated change that may impact the programme to allow informed decision-making.

Which PEB manufacturers do you work with?

We are established channel partners with six leading PEB manufacturers: Kirby Building Systems, Interarch Building Products, Alfa PEB, Fabex, Phenix and Zamil Steel. This multi-partner approach allows us to select the optimal manufacturer for each project based on technical requirements, budget, delivery schedule and the client's brand preference. We do not restrict you to a single OEM.

How long does a typical 5000 m² greenfield warehouse take from contract to handover?

A standard 5000 m² single-span warehouse on a clear site with installation approval in place typically takes 16–22 weeks from contract execution to handover. The programme depends on foundation complexity, PEB lead time, site access and weather. We provide a detailed baseline programme at tender stage and manage progress against it weekly. Concurrent working of civil and structural trades — a core advantage of our integrated construction model — is key to achieving these timelines.
