FAQs — PEB Construction in Mumbai

How do PEB buildings handle Mumbai's heavy monsoon loads?

Our PEB structures for Mumbai are designed per IS:875 standards for Mumbai's wind zone and rainfall intensity. Roof designs include proper slope for drainage, lapped joint sealing, anti-condensation insulation, and gutter sizing for Mumbai's high-intensity rainfall — ensuring zero leakage through monsoon.

Can you build warehouses in Bhiwandi that qualify as Grade-A logistics facilities?

Yes. We installation and build logistics warehouses that meet Grade-A specifications — minimum 10-metre clear height (up to 15 metres), FM2 floor, 24/7 power provisions, ESFR sprinkler-compatible roof, dock levellers, and compliance with Maharashtra fire safety norms. Suitable for 3PL operators, e-commerce companies, and FMCG majors.

What is the typical project timeline for a PEB warehouse in Bhiwandi?

A 50,000 sq.ft. warehouse in Bhiwandi typically takes 12–20 weeks from foundation completion to structural handover with roofing. This includes PEB installation finalisation (3–4 weeks), erection (4–6 weeks), and erection (4–6 weeks). Civil foundation runs in parallel with erection to save time.

Yes, we actively serve Navi Mumbai MIDC, Taloja, Rabale, and Mahape for factory shed and industrial building construction. Our teams are experienced with MIDC plot conditions, utility corridor restrictions, and the specific ground conditions of Navi Mumbai's reclaimed areas.
